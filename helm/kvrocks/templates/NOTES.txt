To move the master before maintenance, without losing a write:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec <master pod> -c kvrocks -- /scripts/kvrocks-switchover.sh

Do not use a plain SENTINEL FAILOVER for that: the old master keeps accepting writes for
about a second after the new one is promoted, and those writes are lost.

Replication is asynchronous. If the master crashes, the last writes it acknowledged
but had not sent to a replica yet are lost.
